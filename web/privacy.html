What we collect

  • Account: your username, email address (optional), name (optional) and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never store the password itself.
  • Delivery details you choose to save: name, checkout email, phone and address. Encrypted with a key unique to your account.
  • Your watchlist: the SKUs you monitor and buy, alert settings (encrypted) and an activity log.
  • Discord (if you connect it): your Discord user ID and username, and the sign-in tokens Discord gives us (encrypted) so our bot can add you to the members' server and message you.
  • Connected browsers: the name and version of each browser you connect the extension to.
  • A copy of your card for extension updates (unless you switch it off): your card locked in your browser with a random key that {{APP_NAME}}'s dashboard keeps in that browser, plus its brand, last 4 digits and expiry so we can show which card it is. We never get the key, so we can't open the copy ourselves. See "Your payment card" below.
  • Card backup (only if you turn it on): a copy of your card locked in your browser with a card passphrase you choose, plus its brand, last 4 digits and expiry so we can show which card it is. We never get the passphrase, so we can't open the copy. See "Your payment card" below.
  • Membership: payments are handled by Whop. We keep your Whop membership ID, its status and renewal date, and the Whop username and email Whop sends us — not your card.

What we don't collect

Your payment card. If you save a card in the {{APP_NAME}} extension, it stays in that browser only. It's encrypted with a key that only the extension in that browser has, and is never sent to us in a form we can read. So that updating the extension doesn't lose it, the extension also locks it with a random key that our dashboard keeps in your browser (AES-256-GCM) and sends us only that locked copy: when you connect a new copy of the extension from the dashboard in that browser, it's handed the key and your card is back. That key never leaves your browser, so we can't open the copy ourselves; switch it off (Setup → Delivery & card) to delete the copies and the key. If you choose to back it up to your account (so a new install or another browser can restore it), the extension locks it first with your card passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 rounds, then AES-256-GCM) and sends us only that locked copy. The passphrase never leaves your browser and we can't recover it. Delete the backup at any time (Setup → Delivery & card, or extension → Card settings); it's also deleted with your account. Anyone who can use that browser profile can use it for checkout through {{APP_NAME}}, like a card saved in the browser itself, so remove it (extension → Card settings) on a shared computer. We don't see your store logins or passwords, and we don't track your browsing. The extension only runs on the supported stores' pages, on payment boxes inside those pages, and on {{APP_NAME}} itself. To check your own SKUs at stores that refuse server checks, it loads those SKUs' product pages from your browser about once a minute (you can switch this off).

How we use it

Only to provide {{APP_NAME}} to you: filling in checkout when you ask, sending the alerts you set up, billing, account emails such as password resets, and keeping the service secure. We don't sell your data and don't use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Your data is tied to your login. Other users can never see it. Our admins can see your username, email, membership, Discord username and usage counts to provide support, but not your delivery details, watchlist or activity. They can see how many members watch each SKU in total, not who. Service providers we use: [hosting provider] (servers), Whop (payments), Discord (pings and the members' server), [email provider] (emails).

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Delivery details, alert settings and Discord tokens are encrypted with AES-256 under a key unique to your account. Connections use HTTPS. Logins and browser connections can be revoked at any time.

Your choices

You can see and change your details in your dashboard and download everything we hold about you (Account → Download my data; for a card backup, its brand, last 4 digits and expiry — the locked copy itself stays in your account, where only your extension can open it). If you delete your account, all of your data is deleted, including your encryption key, so backups of it can't be read.
