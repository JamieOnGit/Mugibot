🤝 Pools

A pool shares its leader's watchlist, armed items and drop watches with the members who join it — on their own watchlists, kept in step the moment the leader changes theirs. You lead the pools you make. Members join by accepting on their own dashboard, and only they can switch on checking out with the leader: then the leader's Auto-checkouts start theirs too, on their own account — their checkout browser, their card, their delivery details, their limits — up to the most they set. You see who joined and who checks out with the leader, never what they'd spend.

Blacklist

Products that should never ping. Anything on the blacklist never alerts (in the dashboard or Discord), isn't tracked by the live sweeps, stays off the Monitor lists, and any monitor on it is removed. Choose a store and a SKU for one product, only a store for everything at that store, or only a SKU for that SKU at every store. Members' own watchlists aren't affected.

Add news

What's being released, when, and which stores are dropping it — trading cards, consoles, collectibles, anything that sells out. It's on every member's dashboard (Drops → 📰 News) and, with Post to #news, in the Discord server's #news. With a release date, it's in the morning's 📅 Coming up too (8:31 in Sydney, the next 14 days). See docs/NEWS.md.
