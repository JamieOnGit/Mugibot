                          🖥️ Checkout browser

                          Checking your browsers…
                            Backup on
                            If the checkout browser isn't open (or isn't the one you're using), whichever of your other browsers with the extension is open checks out instead — the one used most recently. Only ever one browser checks out an order. None open: a ⚡ Buy waits up to 2 minutes for one, and opens a new window for the checkout if that browser has none.

                            Your switch and limits

                            Auto-checkout is off
                            Nothing is bought by itself until you switch it on.
                            When an armed item drops

                            Every order is also checked at checkout: the total (delivery included) must be within the item's max price + $40 and your limit above. Anything off — another item in the bag, a declined card, your bank's or the store's check — and it stops and tells you. It never gets past a bot check or your bank's check.

                            Ready to check out?

                              ⚡ Buy from your phone

                              Discord's ⚡ Buy checks out on your computer
                              Press ⚡ Buy on an alert or a DM in Discord — on your phone, anywhere — and your checkout browser opens the store and checks out. With Auto-checkout on it places the order within your limits (up to the price you were shown, or the item's max price); otherwise it fills checkout in and stops at the final button for you. Nothing to sign in to on your phone, and Discord's reply says how it's going.

                              🛡️ A store's checkpoint (JB Hi-Fi's hCaptcha) is a check for the browser that's buying, so solving it on your phone can't let your computer through — and Mugibot never solves it. When your computer stops at one, Discord's reply and your DM give you two buttons: 🖥️ Solve it on my computer opens Chrome Remote Desktop, where the checkout's tab is in front, waiting (set it up once: install it on your computer from that page and sign in to the app on your phone with the same Google account); 📱 Finish on this phone stops your computer's checkout and opens the store on your phone, with the product, for you to pass the check and pay there.

                              🧹 Your bags at each store

                              A store keeps one bag for your checkout browser, and Auto-checkout only ever buys the one item — anything else in that bag stops it there. Empty a store's bag here, from anywhere: your checkout browser opens the bag, removes everything in it (answering the store's "Are you sure?"), checks it's empty and closes the tab. Clear all goes through every store, one at a time. A store where a checkout of yours is going is left alone.

                                  Browser extension

                                  Checking for the extension…

                                  1. Install

                                  Add to Chrome

                                  From the Chrome Web Store. It updates itself, so you never have to replace it. Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave. Then come back to this page and reload it.

                                  Or download it instead (it won't update itself)
                                  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
                                  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
                                  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped …-extension folder.
                                  4. Come back to this page and reload it.

                                  Updating the extension

                                  The copy from the Chrome Web Store updates itself. If yours was downloaded from this page, move to it once:

                                  1. Add to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store.
                                  2. Come back here, reload the page and press Connect this browser below.
                                  3. Save your card in it: click its icon → Add your card.
                                  4. Remove the old copy: its icon shows OLD. Click it → Remove this copy (or at chrome://extensions, press Remove on the Mugibot that isn't from the Chrome Web Store).

                                  From then on, new versions install themselves.

                                  When there's a new version, the extension's icon in Chrome's toolbar shows NEW and its popup has an Update button that brings you here. Updating like this keeps your saved card and connection.

                                  1. Download the latest version and unzip it.
                                  2. Open the new mugibot-extension folder, select everything in it (⌘+A on a Mac, Ctrl+A on Windows) and copy it into the folder your extension was loaded from, replacing the old files. Not sure which folder? Open chrome://extensions → Mugibot → Details: it's under Source.
                                  3. Click the Mugibot icon in Chrome's toolbar → Reload (or the ↻ on Mugibot at chrome://extensions). If it asks, press Connect this browser below.

                                  Or start fresh: at chrome://extensions press Remove on Mugibot, then Load unpacked and choose the new folder, and press Connect this browser below. If the copy you removed was 1.20 or newer, your card comes back by itself as it connects — no passphrase, nothing to type (Delivery & card, below, says so). From an older copy, copy the files over instead (above) to keep your card, or add it again once. Your delivery details are kept in your account either way.

                                  2. Connect it to your account

                                  The extension only ever sees your account: your SKUs, your details, your ⚡ Buy codes.

It never tries to get past one.
